**Problems in the source:**
- **The `+` on `#時効取得` (L34) looks like a slip for `!`.** It is the only mark that doesn't match. A requirement under it, 「二十年間」, is marked `!` by the author, and the conclusion itself is 「時効取得できない」.
- **「Ｂ@売主」 (L15) looks like a slip for A.** Under Art. 559 the contractor A is the seller. A trial run with the correction (alt-sellerA) gives the same conclusion.
- **Opposing circumstances listed as support:** L6 (residents' objections) and L39 (the memorandum) both point the other way but sit in the same AND as the reasons for the conclusion.
- **Contested legal steps:** using the proviso of Art. 636 for a duty to confirm, and the framing under the proviso of Art. 415(1), are open to dispute. I marked both doubtful.

**Question 2 turns entirely on the start date of the twenty years.** Counting from the registration date (Reiwa 9, April 1), and excluding the first day, the twenty years could be complete. The trial run alt-twenty shows that if that is proved, prescription succeeds.

**Tool bug:** writing `failure` with the full atom wording (「…〔FがEに対して乙不動産につき〕」) is silently ignored. `Compiler.is_failure` only compares against the bare conclusion text, even though the spec says the full wording works. That is why the first verify failed. I worked around it with the bare text (「時効取得できない」), but `solve` then prints a false "文言が理論に見当たりません" warning. `effect` goes through the same code, so it probably has the same bug. Separately, `doubtful` on an application with no evaluation (e.g. 「平穏」) doesn't appear anywhere in the report output. Both are written up in the report's 「道具について」 section; I haven't fixed `mext.py`.
