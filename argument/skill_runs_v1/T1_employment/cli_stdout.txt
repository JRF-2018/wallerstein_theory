**My judgments (`[ai]`)**
- I found no need for same / neg / failure / effect lines; the default readings are correct.
- Six evaluations are rated ok, and one is doubtful: the periphery's "外資の撤退ハザード0.25…@いつでも引き上げられる". A 25%-per-year exit hazard does not mean the capital can leave "at any time", so the wording overstates what the requirement needs.

**Problems with the substance**
- **Is to ought:** the exception is grounded in a "de facto norm", yet it overrides the statutory principle and concludes with "should not". The simulation only shows a tendency. Nothing in the theory says why a de facto norm should take priority over the law.
- **Share to blanket rule:** in sim2, about 51% of stable periphery jobs become non-regular, so about half stay regular. That doesn't support "should not" for stable jobs in general.
- **Merged fact:** "需要の変動にさらされない職である" is the same wording in all three regions, so it becomes one shared atom. Adding a contrary fact for one region would knock down the principle in all three. I suggest `build_argument.py` put the region name into that wording.
