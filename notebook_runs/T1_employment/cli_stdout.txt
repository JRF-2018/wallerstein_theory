**My judgments**

- I marked 7 applications ok and 1 doubtful: "0.497 are non-regular → regular is not the norm in foreign-capital jobs". About half the jobs are still regular. The requirement also nearly restates the conclusion, so it is close to circular.

**Trial runs**
- **Rejecting that doubtful evaluation:** the principle applies again in the periphery. An opponent can overturn the conclusion just by disputing this one evaluation.
- **Adding facts that region U is peripheral and its foreign capital can exit:** region U also falls under the exception. The structural requirements alone are enough to reverse the conclusion.

**Substantive issues**
- **Weak requirement:** the argument would be sturdier if the "regular is no longer the norm" requirement were dropped or backed by a stronger fact (for example, a majority non-regular).
- **De facto norm over legal principle:** the exception rests on a de facto norm, yet it overrides a principle grounded in Labour Contract Act art. 18. That holds if the argument describes how things are. As a claim about how things ought to be, the reason for the priority isn't given.
